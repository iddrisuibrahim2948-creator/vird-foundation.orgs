ABOUT BARAKA

One Community. Integrated Solutions. Lasting Change.

BARAKA represents blessing, benefit, abundance and goodness. The VIRD BARAKA Community Transformation Programme is VIRD Foundation's flagship integrated community development platform, bringing multiple sectors of support into a single community at the same time — rather than isolated, one-off interventions.

THE BARAKA MODEL

Six integrated dimensions of community transformation

Essential Services

Water, health, education and sanitation infrastructure a community can rely on.

Human Capital

Education, health and nutrition investments that build long-term capability.

Livelihoods

Agriculture, enterprise and skills that generate sustainable income.

Social Development

Women, youth and child protection woven through every intervention.

Islamic Community Development

Mosques, Qur'an education and Islamic social finance.

Resilience

Environmental and climate measures that protect long-term gains.
